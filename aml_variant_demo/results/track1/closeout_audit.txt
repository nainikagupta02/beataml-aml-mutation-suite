Scope and replicate framing
---------------------------

This is a high-confidence exonic whole-exome benchmark from one SEQC2 replicate
pair: WES_LL_T_1 (HCC1395 tumor) and WES_LL_N_1 (HCC1395BL matched normal).
HCC1395 is a breast-cancer reference cell line. The result validates this local
workflow; it is neither a targeted-panel caller result nor an AML-specific
performance estimate.

Region gate
-----------

The IMPACT candidate region contained 44 truth SNVs and one truth indel (45
combined), below the configured minimum of 200. A first all-basic-exon fallback
covered 151,135,889 bases and was rejected as broader than a defensible coding
exome. The final benchmark used GENCODE v50 basic protein-coding exons
intersected with the SEQC2 high-confidence BED: 225,074 intervals, 96,549,992
bases, 1,405 SNVs, and 51 indels in truth. The abandoned 151 Mb trial was not
used for the final read slices or metrics.
