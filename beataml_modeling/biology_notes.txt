Biological interpretation (exploratory, based on development-only fitted models):

FLT3-ITD: the largest absolute elastic-net coefficients are CFH (+0.233),
APOL4 (+0.216), FAM124A (+0.178), MIR155HG (+0.140), and SOCS2 (+0.134).
SOCS2 is a literature-supported FLT3-related signal. MIR155HG is also supported:
primary AML experiments reported increased MIR155HG/miR-155 in FLT3-ITD samples
(https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5465836/).
SOCS2, CCNA1 and IL2RA also carry positive coefficients in the small marker model,
consistent with the studies cited in README.md. DUSP6 has a small negative
conditional marker coefficient despite reports of upregulation; this is not
evidence against those studies and illustrates correlated-predictor instability.
CFH, APOL4 and FAM124A are novel-to-this-analysis candidates whose specific
FLT3-predictive biology has not been established by this review, not claims of
new biological discoveries. The forest also prioritizes FAM124A, MIR155HG and CFH.

NPM1: largest absolute elastic-net coefficients include WNT7B (+0.328), NPTX1
(+0.260), HOXB-AS3 (+0.203), HIPK2 (+0.138), SCRN1 (-0.137), CD34 (-0.126),
HMGA2 (-0.122), PBX3 (+0.113), MIR503HG (-0.110) and HOXB3 (+0.107).
HOXB-AS3 has direct experimental support in NPM1-mutated AML
(https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-13259-2).
Low CD34 and a HOX-associated program are consistent with established NPM1
expression findings
(https://ashpublications.org/blood/article/106/12/3747/109726/Mutations-in-nucleophosmin-NPM1-in-acute-myeloid).
The marker model has positive HOXA10, MEIS1, HOXB3 and HOXB6 coefficients,
supporting the expected program; conditional HOXA9 and HOXB2 coefficients are
negative. HOXD4 was removed by the development-fitted expression/variance filter.
In the broad elastic-net fit, HOXA9, HOXA10 and MEIS1 were eligible but received
zero coefficients, while HOXB-AS3, PBX3 and HOXB3 ranked highly. This is compatible
with redundant correlated signals and should not be described as direct
selection of every canonical HOX/MEIS gene. WNT7B and NPTX1 are follow-up
candidates here, not newly validated NPM1 biomarkers.
The NPM1 forest prioritizes LMX1B, HOXB-AS3, HOXB4, WNT7B, HOXB6 and PBX3,
and many additional HOX genes appear among its top 20, supporting a coherent
HOX-associated expression program rather than an isolated single-gene finding.

These gene-level interpretations were added after model selection and did not
change panels, hyperparameters, thresholds, splits or model selection. Both
linear fits are sparse (93 nonzero genes for FLT3-ITD; 88 for NPM1), but sparse
does not mean stable. No causal inference or independent validation was done.
